## Interpretation

Only 564 optimization targets guided thousands of alternatives. Better selection KLD did not yield a general held-out PPL improvement. The report retains those negative outcomes, the retrospective historical-control selection rule, and the lack of a reserved non-final stability estimate. No statistical significance or blind-holdout claim is made.

Complete first-pass coverage does not establish a coordinate-wise local optimum after later accepted changes. A second pass was not run; it is not required for the accepted minimum of one exhaustive pass per recipe.
